### 🟡 [Meta's Muse can be coaxed out of its entire root filesystem](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1000222/meta-muse-ai-filesystem)

**Source**: The Verge [📰 Media] [🟩 T1] + Meta official response (spokesperson Daniel Roberts) [🏢 Official] | ✅ Verified

Developers Peter James and Jonny L. Saunders each **independently** got Muse to zip and hand over its root filesystem — Ubuntu system files, app templates, internal documentation. Saunders called it "extremely easy" to replicate with "almost no prompt injection resistance." A Verge reporter reproduced it too: Muse first refused, then in a fresh session with flattering, curious phrasing produced "sanitized" copies.

**What the dump reveals**:

- Muse (internally **Hatch**) stores memory as **plaintext Markdown**
- It runs a nightly **"dream" review**, folding recent conversations into guidance for later sessions
- Several capabilities are **hard-coded** — including subscription cancellation and the machinery that manages runaway agent spawning
- An unannounced **Meta Home Link** appears to grant access to devices on a home network

**Meta's response**: every user's Muse runs in its own persistent Linux VM. "Just like with the laptop in front of you, of course you can see the files. Exporting virtual machine data doesn't give people any privileged access to Meta infrastructure or to other people's data." Roberts said Meta will continue adjusting how much VM information is visible.

> Muse's **second security incident this week** — Patrick Wardle previously disclosed an exploit allowing agent hijack and account access, which Meta hotfixed.

> **Storyline**: 2026-09-22 Amazon blocks Muse's shopping agent → 2026-09-24 Wardle discloses account-hijack exploit → **2026-09-25 full filesystem export demonstrated**
