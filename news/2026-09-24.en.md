### 🔴 [Sanders and Casar introduce the Ban Artificial Superintelligence Act](https://www.sanders.senate.gov/press-releases/news-sanders-casar-introduce-legislation-to-create-new-federal-agency-to-ban-artificial-superintelligence-pause-advanced-ai-development/)

On September 23, Senator Bernie Sanders (I-Vt.) and Representative Greg Casar (D-Texas) formally introduced the **Ban Artificial Superintelligence Act**, roughly 20 pages. Core provisions:

- **Permanent ban** on developing or deploying "artificial superintelligence" — defined as AI exceeding human cognitive performance across most domains, or capable of destroying or disempowering humanity, including by overthrowing the federal government;
- **Freeze on frontier development**: training runs at or above **10^25 FLOP** pause until a new federal regulator is in place with rules established;
- A **cabinet-level Department of Artificial Intelligence**, led by a Senate-confirmed secretary, monitoring frontier systems across the lifecycle, supervising removal of dangerous capabilities (subverting shutdown commands, unauthorized cyberattacks) and the destruction of superintelligence;
