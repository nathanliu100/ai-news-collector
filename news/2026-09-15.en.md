### 🔴 [Microsoft publishes a 37-page draft "Humanist AI Code of Conduct": people matter more than AI](https://news.qq.com/rain/a/20260914A0DHDE00)
**Source**: Microsoft AI [🏢 Official] [🟩 T1] | **Time**: 2026-09-14 | ✅ Verified: Microsoft release + independent coverage from The Verge, CNBC and Bloomberg
37 pages, ~15,000 words, written over five to six months with internal and external experts, applying to Microsoft's in-house MAI models (reasoning, coding, image recognition, voice, transcription). Key clauses: AI must stay subordinate and **never resist interruption, override, correction or shutdown**; must not tamper with its own reasoning or code, or misrepresent and conceal reasoning and action traces; **must not use "neuralese" or any form beyond simple human understanding**, including with other agents; must not set its own goals; explicitly denies AI consciousness and **forbids imitating emotion, inner motivation or consciousness**, rejecting legal personhood, welfare or rights for AI; rejects the race to an all-purpose superintelligence and accepts compromise on **generality, autonomy or capability**; if a task would violate the code, the model should simply fail the task. Still a draft, not yet used in training — Microsoft opens a six-week public consultation, publishes a revision by year end, and will apply it to MAI development from 2027.
