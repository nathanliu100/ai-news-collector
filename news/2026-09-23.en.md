### 🟡 [Tencent ARC open-sources WorldCrafter: a camera-queryable 3D memory for video world models](https://github.com/TencentARC/WorldCrafter)
**Source**: Tencent ARC + Peking University [🏢 Official] [🟩 T1] | **Date**: 9/23 | ✅ Verified: official GitHub repo + arXiv paper + Hugging Face weights
It targets the classic interactive-video failure: over long horizons and across camera moves, the generator forgets what it already showed. WorldCrafter learns an **implicit 3D-aware memory in which the requested viewpoint decides how multi-view evidence is compressed into the generator's token budget** — no explicit depth estimation or geometric warping. Combined with recent temporal context and few-step distillation, it supports streaming exploration from a single image or text prompt. Evaluated on 145 scenes and 725 camera trajectories, ahead of baselines on long-horizon revisit consistency and camera-control accuracy, with the highest overall VBench score. Ships Base and a distilled Fast variant. [💎 Dept relevant]
