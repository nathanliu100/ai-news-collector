### 🔴 [ShengShu Releases Vidu S2: Edit Video While It Plays — Style, Outfit, Character, Background](https://vidu.com/vidu-stream)
**Source**: ShengShu official + arXiv technical report [🏢 Official] [🟩 T1] | **Time**: 2026-09-15 | ✅ Verified: arXiv:2609.11638 + vidu.com public demo + QbitAI / NetEase Tech independent coverage

Just **69 days** after S1, Vidu S2 opened to everyone on day one. Two models: **S2-Editing** performs real-time editing on a running video stream — style transfer, virtual try-on, character swap, background swap — using frame-aligned sparse attention so each output frame locks to the same instant in the source, keeping motion trajectories intact. **S2-Avatar** jumps from 540P to **720P at 25-42 FPS**, accepts new reference images (a cup, a garment, a scene) mid-generation, and uses a VLM Agent to keep checking frames and maintaining state like "still holding the cup." The core technique is Self-Replay Forcing: generate a long rollout the way inference actually runs, then sample and re-noise clips from that self-generated trajectory for causal replay, **teaching the model to recover from its own errors**. Benchmarks: S2-Avatar takes all nine metrics on StreamAV-Bench (subject consistency 0.998); S2-Editing scores 3.74 on Sparkle-Bench and 4.26 on the combined OpenVE + RefVIE eval, both ahead of the offline comparison model Bernini-R 14B. It also explores **spatial video**, converting live output into synchronized left/right eye views for VR headsets. [💎 Dept relevant] [🎯 Focus tool]

> 7/3 S1 paper (540P real-time dialogue) → 9/10 S2 paper → **9/15 S2 released with full public access**

## 🔭 Editor's Note

What's worth remembering today isn't any single model release — it's that **voice got pushed from two directions on the same day**. Google put Gemini 3.8 Live inside Workspace and Search, solving "how do voice agents land inside enterprises"; StepFun laid out five StepAudio 3 models at once, solving "how does a Chinese developer get a complete voice stack in one integration." But both sets of numbers expose the same thing: **first place on a speech quality leaderboard (82.6) and one-in-three completion on banking tasks (35.1%) belong to the same model.** Sounding human is largely solved. Getting things done is not. And the industry is pouring resources into the former because it demos better.

Second, **AI safety moved from voluntary corporate action to corporations actively asking to be regulated.** Lehane's "no antitrust waiver needed" carries the most information per word of anything said today — it means all three labs' counsel have judged the political upside of coordinating to exceed the legal risk. But Recht's challenge in POLITICO cuts just as deep: **if the evaluation orgs and the labs they evaluate come out of the same doctoral programs and share one worldview, independence is ornament.** OpenAI itself said assessors "must be seen as independent from us," which means it knows. The question isn't whether a standards body forms — it's **who funds it and who can veto its membership.**

Third, **domestic design and asset tools are converging on real-time.** Vidu S2's 69-day cycle, Lovart's multi-angle synthesis and Viggle's three-step character swap all compress the delay between idea and visible result. For teams producing promo assets, this means **asset production is shifting from batch processing to interactive iteration** — generate twenty and pick one becomes adjust while watching. That change in how we work will touch your day more than any benchmark.

One last observation: today's GitHub trending top five is almost entirely **agent governance** — version control for agents (atlas), rule systems for agents (agent-skills), turning coding agents into research agents (OpenResearch). Not a single new model. **When an ecosystem starts building tools for keeping agents in line, agents have passed the "can we" phase and entered "how do we."**
